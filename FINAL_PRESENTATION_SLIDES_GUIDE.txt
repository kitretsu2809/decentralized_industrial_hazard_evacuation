  Facility Graph Representation & Three Industrial Benchmark Topologies

GRAPH FORMULATION:
  • Facility Graph: G = (V, E)
    - V: Vertices representing intersections, corridors, rooms, stairwells, and muster exits.
    - E: Edges representing walkable corridors with physical length d_ij and width w_ij.
  • Physical Topology Masking Matrix (M_ij ∈ {0, 1}):
    - M_ij = 1 if a physically unobstructed corridor connects node i and node j.
    - M_ij = 0 if separated by walls, machinery, or structural barriers.
    - Mathematical Benefit: Masks out invalid attention weights, preventing spurious edge routing.

EIGHT-DIRECTION DETERMINISTIC COMPASS SLOTS:
  • Every corridor at a junction is assigned to one of 8 compass slots:
    [0: N,  1: NE,  2: E,  3: SE,  4: S,  5: SW,  6: W,  7: NW]
  • Vertical stairwells are mapped deterministically (Up = North, Down = South).
  • Guarantees that action index, signboard arrows, and communication bitmasks share identical indexing.

THE THREE SELECTABLE INDUSTRIAL BENCHMARK TOPOLOGIES:
  1. Petrochemical Refinery (`petrochemical`):
     - 3 floors, 36 junctions/rooms, 53 conduits, 4 muster assembly exits.

CONTINUOUS SOCIAL FORCE MODEL (SFM) — HELBING FORMULATION:
  m_i * (d v_i / dt) = F_i^goal + F_i^wall + ∑_{j ≠ i} F_ij^rep + F_i^flee

  1. Goal-Directed Driving Force (F_i^goal):
     F_i^goal = m_i * (v_0 * e_i^des - v_i) / τ
     Pedestrians steer toward a lookahead point P_i^look along corridor centerline vector u.

  2. Corridor Boundary Force (F_i^wall):
     Exponential repulsion from corridor walls preventing clipping and unnatural cornering.

  3. Inter-Pedestrian Repulsion (F_ij^rep):
     Socio-psychological and physical contact forces causing panic crushing and arching at doorways.

  4. Hazard Repulsion Force (F_i^flee):
     F_i^flee = - ∇ H(x, y) — pedestrians actively recoil from visible smoke and flames.

WEIDMANN VELOCITY-DENSITY BOTTLENECK CURVE:
  v(ρ) = v_0 * [1 - exp(-γ * (1/ρ - 1/ρ_jam))]
  As crowd density ρ exceeds 3.5 persons/m², pedestrian velocity drops toward zero, modeling fatal crush.

ISO 13571 FRACTIONAL EFFECTIVE DOSE (FED) TOXIC INHALATION:
  FED(t) = ∫_0^t [ C_gas(t') / LC_50 ] dt' + ∫_0^t [ Q_thermal(t') / Q_crit ] dt'
  • When FED ≥ 1.0: Evacuee suffers acute toxic incapacitation (casualty recorded).
  • Realistic gas dispersion modeled via 2D Gaussian advection-diffusion PDEs.

BIT-FIELD PACKET STRUCTURE (Exact 4-Byte PDU Layout):

  ┌────────────────┬────────────────┬────────────────┬──────────┬──────────┬──────────┐
  │ BITS 0 .. 7    │ BITS 8 .. 15   │ BITS 16 .. 23  │ 24 .. 27 │ 28 .. 29 │ 30 .. 31 │
  ├────────────────┼────────────────┼────────────────┼──────────┼──────────┼──────────┤
  │ DIRECTION MASK │ HAZARD SCORE   │ CROWD DENSITY  │ HAZ_TYPE │ VERSION  │ TTL HOPS │
  │ (8 Compass Dir)│ (Quantized H)  │ (Quantized ρ)  │ (4 Bits) │ (2 Bits) │ (2 Bits) │
  │ 1 Byte         │ 1 Byte         │ 1 Byte         │ 4 Bits   │ 2 Bits   │ 2 Bits   │
  └────────────────┴────────────────┴────────────────┴──────────┴──────────┴──────────┘

DETAILED BIT ALLOCATION BREAKDOWN:
  • Bits 0–7 (Safe Corridor Mask):
    One bit for each compass direction [N, NE, E, SE, S, SW, W, NW].
    '1' indicates the corridor is physically walkable and safe; '0' indicates blocked/hazardous.
  • Bits 8–15 (Fused Hazard Score H_v):
    Quantized value: round(min(1.0, H_v) * 255). Resolves 0.004 unit hazard gradations.
  • Bits 16–23 (Crowd Density ρ_v):
    Quantized crowd value: round(min(2.0, ρ_v) / 2.0 * 255).
  • Bits 24–27 (Hazard Classification Code):
    [0: None, 1: Fire, 2: Smoke, 3: Gas/Toxic, 4: Chemical Spill, 5: Collapse, 6: Explosion].
  • Bits 28–29 (Protocol Version): Supports future backward-compatible firmware upgrades (v1).
  • Bits 30–31 (Time-To-Live / Hop Count): Limits flooding to strictly 1-hop or 2-hop local clusters.

EVENT-TRIGGERED TRANSMISSION RULES:
  • An edge node emits a packet ONLY when:
      |H_current - H_last_transmitted| > ε_H (0.05)   OR   |ρ_current - ρ_last_transmitted| > ε_ρ (0.10)
  • Quiescent Heartbeat: 0.1 Hz (one packet per 10 seconds during normal operations).
  • Result: Eliminates 98% of unnecessary network traffic compared to continuous GNN broadcasting!

Byte 0 allocates one bit to each of the eight compass corridor directions. If a corridor becomes blocked

"Now let us examine the neural architecture executing on each edge node: the ST-TBA-GAT model.
Inference proceeds in three stages.

First, the spatial aggregation stage uses a modified GATv2 attention mechanism. Crucially, the attention
coefficients are masked by our physical topology matrix M_ij. Nodes separated by concrete barriers
receive negative infinity attention. On embedded microcontrollers, we replace expensive floating-point
softmax exponents with quantized INT8 Manhattan distance attention scoring.

Second, spatial representations pass into a Gated Recurrent Unit (GRU) with a 64-dimensional hidden
state. As proved in our literature review of the 2025 PLOS ONE paper, spatial attention alone is
'short-sighted.' The GRU gives each sign temporal memory—allowing it to recognize whether a toxic plume
is expanding toward it or receding.

Third, the actor head outputs a distribution over our 37 categorical actions. To prevent dangerous visual
flickering—where signs rapidly alternate between left and right arrows as smoke drifts—we introduced a
bimodal attractor hysteresis filter. A sign will only toggle direction if the new action's probability
exceeds the incumbent action by a significant hysteresis margin."

[DEFENSE Q&A PREPARATION]
• Question: "Why use GATv2 instead of standard GAT?"
• Bulletproof Answer: "In standard GAT (Veličković et al., 2018), the attention mechanism is static:
  the ranking of attention weights does not depend on the query node's state. GATv2 computes dynamic
  attention, meaning an edge sign can adjust which neighbor it pays attention to based on its own
  internal hazard state."

CTDE PARADIGM ARCHITECTURE:
  • Offline Training Phase (Centralized):
    - A Global Centralized Critic V(s_all) has access to the full plant state, including hidden gas

    - Guides policy gradient updates using Generalized Advantage Estimation (GAE).

    - The Global Critic is COMPLETELY DISCARDED.
    - Each edge sign executes its local Actor policy π(a_i | o_i) using ONLY its onboard sensors

  1. Stage 1 (Static Single-Source Fire):

  2. Stage 2 (Dynamic Toxic Gas Dispersion):

  3. Stage 3 (High-Density Stampedes & Chokepoints):
     120 to 250 evacuees. High-density spawning. Policy learns learned flow-splitting to avoid doorway crush.
  4. Stage 4 (Stochastic Link Severance & Multi-Hazard):
     Random 0–50% link cuts, simultaneous fire and gas flare-ups. Policy masters decentralized robustness.

MULTI-OBJECTIVE REWARD FORMULATION:
  R_total = R_evac + R_cong + R_jam + R_hazard + R_time
  • R_evac = +10.0 per survivor reaching a muster point.
  • R_cong = -2.0 * Var(ρ_corridors) (Penalizes uneven crowd distribution across hallways).
  • R_jam  = -5.0 * ∑ max(0, ρ_door - 3.5)² (Severe quadratic penalty for doorway crush density).
  • R_hazard = -50.0 per toxic or thermal casualty (ISO 13571 FED ≥ 1.0).
  • R_time = -0.05 per elapsed simulation timestep (Incentivizes rapid facility clearing).
